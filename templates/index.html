About Fitamis

How it Started

It started when my mom suggested her and I compete in an fitness competition for $50.00, whoever has the most minutes of exercise in a month wins! We would log on a piece of paper. I won the competition and we started a new one for the month of August with my dad. It's way easier to log our minutes on a web app so she asked me to make this, that's how Fitamis came to be.

1 Log the total minutes of exercise onto Fitamis.

2 Compete with friends to win a prize of your choice!
